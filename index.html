Who We Are

About Cosmo eSports

Cosmo eSports is a competitive Fortnite organization built around one idea: talent deserves a clear path to the top. We run seven structured rosters, from Grinder+ all the way up to our Pro roster, so every player - whether they're chasing their first placement or already stacking wins - knows exactly what the next step looks like.

No shortcuts, no politics. Just consistency, reps, and results. We call it Reach Beyond, and it's less a slogan than a standard we hold every grinder in the org to, from our newest Academy signee to our Pro squad.

PLAYER PATHWAY

Seven tiers, one staircase. Every roster exists to get you ready for the next.

COMPETITIVE STANDARDS

Attendance, PR, and in-game conduct are tracked. Consistency earns you your seat.
